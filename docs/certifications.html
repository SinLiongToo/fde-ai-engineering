This page answers two questions: are certifications worth it, and which one; and how to plan 12 weeks of study starting from this course.

Fields the official pages don't state are marked "not listed" rather than filled from third-party sites.

先講立場：證照什麼時候值得考First, a position: when certifications are worth it

對 FDE、AI 應用工程師這類職位，證照的分量通常不如作品（見作品集專案）。它證明你「知道」某些東西，但不證明你「做得出來」。不過，在下面幾種情況，證照有實際用處：

For FDE and AI application engineering roles, certifications usually carry less weight than projects (see portfolio projects). They show you know something, not that you can build it. They do help in a few situations:

客戶綁定某家雲端Customers on one cloud

你的目標產業（例如半導體、製造業）的客戶主要用 AWS、Azure 或 Google Cloud，證照能證明你懂他們的基礎架構。

If your target industry's customers mostly use AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, a certification shows you understand their infrastructure.

履歷還沒有 AI 經歷No AI experience on your CV yet

轉職時，證照可以當成「有系統學過」的門檻證明，讓履歷通過第一關篩選。

When changing careers, a certification proves structured study and can get a CV past first-round screening.

公司或合作夥伴要求Employer or partner requirements

有些顧問公司的合作夥伴資格，要求一定人數的認證人員（例如 Claude Partner Network）。

Some consulting partner programs require a number of certified staff (the Claude Partner Network, for example).

用考試大綱當學習地圖Using the syllabus as a map

就算不考，考試大綱也是很好的「該學什麼」清單。

Even if you never sit the exam, the syllabus is a good "what to learn" checklist.

不建議為了收集證照而犧牲做專案的時間。一張證照加一個有評測數字的作品，比三張證照有說服力。

Don't trade project time for collecting certificates. One certification plus one project with eval numbers beats three certifications.

12 週學習計畫A 12-week study plan

以每週 5 到 8 小時估算。已經有程式基礎的話，第 1 週可以很快帶過。重點是每個階段都有做出來的東西，不是只讀完。

Assumes 5–8 hours a week. With programming experience, week 1 goes quickly. The point is that every phase ends with something built, not just read.

12 週結束時，你會有：兩個附評測數字的作品（其中一個有公開網址）、一份讀完的課程，以及（如果選擇考）一張證照。這三樣放在一起，比任何單一項目都有說服力。

After 12 weeks you'll have two projects with eval numbers (one with a public URL), a completed course, and optionally a certification. Together they're more convincing than any one alone.
